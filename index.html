Profile

About Me

I am Dr. Revanasiddappa Bandi, an Associate Professor in Computer Science and Engineering with more than 15 years of experience in teaching, research, student mentoring and academic activities.

I completed my Ph.D. in IoT and Machine Learning from Visvesvaraya Technological University (VTU), Belagavi, in 2024. My research interests focus on applying AI, ML, IoT and cloud/edge technologies to real-world problems.

My work particularly explores intelligent and data-driven solutions for smart agriculture, computer vision, connected systems and emerging cloud technologies.
